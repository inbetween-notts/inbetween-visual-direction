Idea
A quieter, more premium InBetween — like a beautiful cookbook. Big serif headlines, lots of air, and an arch-shaped photo frame borrowed from Damascene doorways, used throughout as a signature.
Type & colour
Instrument Serif + Instrument Sans. Soft mint #CFE5D9 as the lead, cream paper and deep green-black ink. One Arabic line per page at most. Wordmark in black (or white on dark).
Layout
Editorial index lists instead of cards, numbered sections, an essay-style story page, and quiet underline forms. Placeholders as in A: hours, phone, email, dates and story copy to confirm.

Our story

A little piece of home, on Mansfield Road.

InBetween began with the recipes we grew up with — the cardamom in the coffee, the za'atar on Saturday mornings, the trays of sweets that appear whenever guests arrive. In Syria, food is how you say welcome.

We wanted a small corner of Nottingham where that feeling lives on. Everything on our counter is made in our kitchen, each morning: soft cakes with pistachio, rose and orange blossom, savoury plates with fresh flatbread, and jars from home packed by hand.

"The name is the moment in between — between coffee and conversation, between home and somewhere new."

So pull up a chair. Order a pot of Syrian coffee, try the pistachio cake, and if you're planning something special, tell us about it. That's what we're here for — baked moments, shared stories.
