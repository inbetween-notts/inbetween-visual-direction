CDirection C — Shared Stories · bold & expressive · leads with the pink of the biscuits and Instagram
Idea
The InBetween Instagram turned into a website: loud, warm and fun. A giant wordmark, polaroid snaps with handwritten captions, poster-style event cards and the chalkboard brunch menu, recreated.
Type & colour
Bricolage Grotesque (chunky, friendly) + Caveat for the handwriting. Biscuit pink #E79CBC leads, with mint, butter yellow and slate-black ink. Wordmark in white on pink, black elsewhere.
Layout
Big chunky tiles, stickers, rotated photos, and a playful 30-second cake planner that writes the brief for the customer. Placeholders as in A and B: hours, phone, email, dates and story copy to confirm.

Syrian recipes.
Nottingham kitchen.

بين القهوة والشاي، حكايات تُروى
{{ g.cap }}

InBetween began with the recipes we grew up with — the cardamom in the coffee, the za'atar on Saturday mornings, the trays of sweets that appear whenever guests arrive. In Syria, food is how you say welcome.

So we opened a little place on Mansfield Road where that feeling lives on. Everything on the counter is baked in our kitchen each morning. The name? It's the moment in between — coffee and conversation, home and somewhere new.
