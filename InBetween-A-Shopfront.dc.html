Idea
The website as the shop itself: slate-green frontage, warm white walls, the mint menu board on the wall. Familiar, calm, easy to use. A conventional layout that any customer finds their way around.
Type & colour
Marcellus (echoes the menu-board capitals) + Jost for reading. Slate #39433F, warm white, mint menu-board panel. Wordmark set in black or white, as on the biscuits — a stand-in until the real logo file arrives.

Opening hours, phone (0115 496 0000), email, workshop dates and story copy are drafts to confirm with the owner. Striped boxes = photos still needed. No prices on cakes, catering or workshops — everything is quoted.

Home · Our story

Recipes from home, baked on Mansfield Road.

InBetween is a small Syrian café and bakery in Nottingham — a place for good coffee, homemade cakes and the kind of welcome we grew up with.

Where it began

InBetween began with the recipes we grew up with — the cardamom in the coffee, the za'atar on Saturday mornings, the trays of sweets that appear whenever guests arrive. In Syria, food is how you say welcome.

We wanted a small corner of Nottingham where that feeling lives on. Everything on our counter is made in our kitchen, each morning: soft cakes with pistachio, rose and orange blossom, savoury plates with fresh flatbread, and jars from home packed by hand.

The name is the moment in between — between coffee and conversation, between home and somewhere new. Baked moments, shared stories.

بين القهوة والشاي، حكايات تُروى
"Between coffee and tea, stories are told." — from the print on our wall.
